# Brightfield Marketing: KPI Definitions and Targets

*Fictional company. Targets are made up for a training session.*

## Funnel definitions

| Stage | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **Lead** | Anyone who gives us their contact details: demo request, content download, event sign-up, webinar registration. |
| **MQL** (marketing-qualified lead) | A lead from a UK business with 20 to 500 employees, in a finance or payroll role. |
| **SQL** (sales-qualified lead) | An MQL that sales has spoken to and agreed has a real need and a budget this year. |
| **Deal won** | A signed contract. |
| **Revenue** | First-year contract value of deals won. |

## How we calculate the key numbers

| Metric | Formula |
| --- | --- |
| Cost per lead (CPL) | Spend ÷ Leads |
| Lead-to-SQL rate | SQLs ÷ Leads |
| Cost per deal | Spend ÷ Deals won |
| Return on spend | Revenue ÷ Spend |

Organic search and email have no media spend, so we report their leads and revenue but leave out cost metrics.

## Targets for this financial year

| Metric | Target | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cost per lead (paid channels) | Under £120 | Blended across all paid channels |
| Lead-to-SQL rate | 18% or higher | Quality matters more than volume |
| Return on spend (paid channels) | 5× or higher | Measured quarterly |
| Deals won per month | 25 or more | All channels |

## What leadership cares about most

1. Revenue and return on spend.
2. Lead quality, measured by lead-to-SQL rate.
3. Cost per lead, but only alongside quality. Cheap leads that never convert are not a win.
