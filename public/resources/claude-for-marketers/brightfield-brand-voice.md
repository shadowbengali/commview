# Brightfield Software: Brand Voice Guide

*Fictional company. Made up for a training session.*

## Who we are

Brightfield makes payroll software for UK small and mid-sized businesses (20 to 500 employees). We help finance teams run payroll in hours, not days, and stay on the right side of HMRC.

## Who we talk to

- **Finance directors and finance managers.** Busy, sceptical of hype, care about accuracy, compliance and time saved.
- **Payroll managers.** Hands-on users. Care about year-end, pension auto-enrolment, RTI submissions and not being the person who got payroll wrong.

## How we sound

| We are | We are not |
| --- | --- |
| Plain and direct | Jargon-heavy or salesy |
| Calm and confident | Alarmist about compliance |
| Specific (numbers, dates, deadlines) | Vague ("powerful", "seamless", "cutting-edge") |
| Warm and human | Jokey or casual about money |

## Rules

1. Use British English: organise, colour, programme, "payroll run", not "pay run".
2. Lead with the reader's problem, then how we solve it.
3. Use real numbers wherever we can: hours saved, deadlines, penalties avoided.
4. Short sentences. One idea per sentence.
5. No emoji in anything customer-facing.
6. Never use: "game-changer", "revolutionary", "seamless", "leverage", "synergy", "unlock".
7. Always say "HMRC", never "the taxman".
8. Calls to action are one clear verb: "Book a demo", "Download the guide", "Register now".

## Example

**Not like this:** "Unlock seamless payroll with our revolutionary platform! 🚀"

**Like this:** "Year-end is 5 April. Brightfield checks every payslip before it goes to HMRC, so your final submission is right first time."
