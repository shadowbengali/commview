# Brightfield Monthly Marketing Report: Template

*Fictional company. Made up for a training session.*

Every monthly report follows this structure. Audience: the leadership team. Length: one page, under 400 words.

## 1. Headline numbers

Three numbers only, each compared with last month and with target:

- Revenue from marketing-sourced deals
- Return on paid spend
- Lead-to-SQL rate

## 2. What changed and why

Two or three short points. Explain the cause, not just the movement.
Example: "LinkedIn cost per lead rose 18% because we paused the cheaper retargeting audience. Lead quality held at 25% to SQL."

## 3. Channel scorecard

A short table: channel, spend, leads, cost per lead, lead-to-SQL rate, revenue. Flag any channel more than 20% off target.

## 4. What we're doing next month

Three actions at most, each with an owner and a date.

## 5. Risks or decisions needed

Anything leadership needs to decide or know about. If nothing, say "None this month."
