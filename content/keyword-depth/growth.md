## ADD: H2 targeting `b2b marketing problems`

### Where most B2B marketing goes wrong

B2B marketing rarely fails because a business is missing one more channel.

The more common problem is that the parts do not connect. The positioning says one thing, paid media targets another audience, content answers questions nobody close to buying is asking, sales has a different definition of a good lead, and reporting stops at clicks or form fills.

That creates activity without a reliable commercial system.

Typical B2B marketing problems include:

- **Unclear positioning:** buyers cannot quickly understand who the business is for, what problem it solves or why it is different.
- **Weak ICP discipline:** campaigns reach companies that can engage but are unlikely to buy, expand or stay.
- **Disconnected channels:** SEO, paid media, content, outbound and email operate as separate programmes rather than supporting the same buyer journey.
- **Sales and marketing misalignment:** the teams disagree about lead quality, qualification, ownership or what should happen next.
- **Too much top-of-funnel measurement:** traffic, impressions and leads rise without enough visibility of qualified conversations, opportunities, pipeline and revenue.
- **No operating rhythm:** teams launch campaigns but do not have a consistent way to learn, prioritise and move budget toward what is working.

The first job is therefore not to prescribe more marketing. It is to identify where the commercial system is breaking down.

That is why Commview starts with the problem, then decides which combination of growth work is actually required.

## ADD: H2 targeting `b2b marketing agency engagement`

### How a B2B growth marketing engagement works

The engagement should fit the problem rather than force the problem into a standard agency package.

Some businesses need a focused project: diagnose why organic growth has stalled, rebuild measurement, fix a conversion journey or establish an AEO baseline.

Others need ongoing growth leadership and execution across several connected workstreams.

Commview can work on a project, retained or embedded basis. In each case the operating principle is the same:

1. **Diagnose:** establish what is happening using commercial data, customer evidence, search demand, channel performance and the people closest to the problem.
2. **Prioritise:** decide which constraint is worth addressing first and what success should look like.
3. **Deliver:** work alongside the team to implement the change rather than stopping at recommendations.
4. **Measure:** track whether the work changed buyer behaviour, pipeline, cost, speed or another agreed business outcome.
5. **Adapt:** use the evidence to decide what should happen next.

That means the scope can cross SEO, AEO, demand generation, paid media, content, conversion and GTM when the evidence says the problem crosses those boundaries.

It also means we will not recommend a channel simply because it is a service we can sell.
