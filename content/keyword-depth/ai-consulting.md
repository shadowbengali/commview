## ADD/REFINE: H2 targeting `ai for business`

### AI for business should start with the work, not the tool

AI is useful when it changes how work gets done.

That might mean reducing repetitive manual effort, shortening a process, improving access to information, increasing capacity or helping a team make a better-informed decision. If the use case cannot be connected to an operational outcome, adopting another AI tool is unlikely to solve much.

Commview starts with the workflow and the constraint.

What is taking too long? Where is skilled time being spent on repeatable work? Where are people copying information between systems? Which decisions are slowed down because the right context is difficult to assemble? Where does quality vary because the process is inconsistent?

Only then do we decide whether AI belongs in the answer.

That keeps AI for business grounded in operations rather than demonstrations.

## ADD/REFINE: H2 targeting `ai for business operations`

### Where AI earns its place in business operations

Operational AI works best where there is a repeatable job, usable data, a clear boundary around the task and a way to judge the output.

Examples can include:

- **Reporting:** assembling recurring data, identifying changes and producing a first analytical view for human review.
- **Content operations:** supporting research, drafting, adaptation, classification and production workflows where quality controls are defined.
- **Sales intelligence:** bringing account, interaction and opportunity context together so a person can prepare or prioritise faster.
- **Workflow automation:** connecting systems and automating defined steps while escalating exceptions to people.
- **Knowledge and decision support:** retrieving internal information, summarising evidence and helping teams interrogate a body of approved material.

Not every process should be automated. High-risk decisions, poor-quality source data, unclear ownership and tasks requiring substantial judgement can make automation slower or riskier rather than better.

The point is to find the work where AI can earn its place, then measure whether it did.

### AI consulting services built around implementation

Commview's AI consulting services cover the path from identifying a useful opportunity to getting it into day-to-day work.

Find and prioritise automation opportunities based on the work being done, the value available and the practical constraints around implementation.

Link: [/ai-consulting/automation](/ai-consulting/automation)

#### AI implementation consulting

Move from a chosen use case into requirements, build, testing, deployment, adoption and measurement.

Link: [/ai-consulting/implementation](/ai-consulting/implementation)

#### Generative AI consulting

Apply generative AI to suitable tasks such as drafting, extraction, summarisation and classification, with evaluation and controls appropriate to the use case.

Link: [/ai-consulting/genai](/ai-consulting/genai)

#### AI transformation

Build a phased operating model for businesses where AI affects several teams or workflows, including prioritisation, governance, adoption and measurement.

The technology can vary. The standard is whether the implementation improves the work.
