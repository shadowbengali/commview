These marks indicate organisations where people in the Commview network gained experience. They are **not** presented as Commview clients, partners, sponsors or endorsers.

Keep the strip subordinate to the copy. Use consistent visual height, preserve clear space and aspect ratios, and do not recolour, stretch or distort marks.
