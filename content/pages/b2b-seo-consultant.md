# B2B SEO Consultant for Long Sales Cycles and Complex Buyers

B2B SEO is not consumer SEO with smaller search volumes.

The buyer can be a committee. The sales cycle can take months. A keyword with 40 searches can be commercially more useful than one with 4,000. And ranking is only valuable if the people arriving are the people your business can actually sell to.

Commview combines SEO with GTM judgement so search strategy is built around commercial demand, not a traffic graph.

## Why B2B SEO is different

B2B buyers rarely move from a generic search to a purchase in one session.

Different people research the problem, requirements, category, vendors, alternatives, integrations, risk and proof. The search strategy therefore has to support a buying process rather than chase isolated keywords.

For many B2B businesses, the addressable market is also relatively small. That makes relevance more important. Ten visits from the right accounts can matter more than hundreds from people who will never buy.

The job of B2B SEO is to create useful visibility at the moments that influence the commercial journey.

## How to prioritise a B2B keyword strategy

Search volume is an input, not the decision.

We look at buyer intent, ICP fit, commercial proximity, ranking feasibility and the role a query plays in the buying journey. Bottom-of-funnel opportunities often deserve attention first because they reveal existing demand: service, category, comparison, alternative, integration and high-intent problem searches.

Then we build outward.

That means a low-volume term with strong buyer fit can outrank a high-volume informational term in the plan. The objective is not to own the largest spreadsheet of keywords. It is to own useful demand.

## B2B SEO FAQ

### How is B2B SEO different from B2C SEO?

B2B SEO usually deals with longer buying cycles, multiple stakeholders, smaller addressable audiences and higher-value decisions. That changes keyword selection and content strategy. Commercial relevance and buyer fit can matter more than raw volume, while content often needs to support research, comparison, validation and internal decision-making before a lead appears.

### How long until B2B SEO produces pipeline?

There is no credible universal timeline. It depends on current authority, technical health, competition, the queries targeted and how quickly changes ship. Existing high-intent pages can improve faster than a new content programme. We measure leading indicators early, but judge the programme by useful commercial outcomes over time.

### What does a B2B SEO consultant actually do?

A B2B SEO consultant connects search demand to the commercial model. That includes technical diagnosis, keyword and competitor research, page strategy, content planning, on-page optimisation, internal linking, authority signals, AEO and measurement. The important distinction is prioritisation: deciding which search opportunities are actually worth the business pursuing.
