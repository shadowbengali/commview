# Answer Engine Optimization Services

Answer Engine Optimisation services should do more than add FAQ schema and mention AI.

Commview helps B2B businesses understand how their expertise is represented across AI-assisted discovery, improve the content and technical signals machines can interpret, and measure whether the work changes visibility.

The programme connects audit, strategy, content, technical AEO and measurement rather than treating AI visibility as a standalone trick.

## What our AEO services deliver

We start with what buyers are likely to ask and what answer engines can currently understand about your business.

A typical engagement moves through five connected areas:

1. **Audit:** establish current visibility, citations, source patterns and content gaps.
2. **Strategy:** prioritise the questions, entities, topics and commercial journeys that matter.
3. **Content:** improve existing pages and create missing material that answers those needs clearly.
4. **Technical:** strengthen crawlability, structure, entity clarity and machine-readable context.
5. **Measurement:** track visibility and citations over time alongside conventional search and commercial metrics.

AEO does not replace SEO. It extends the job of making a business understandable and useful wherever buyers research.

## AEO audit

An AEO audit establishes a baseline.

We examine representative buyer questions across relevant AI and search experiences, identify whether your brand or expertise appears, look at the sources being surfaced and compare that with the content and entities on your own site.

The useful output is not a vanity “AI visibility score”. It is a prioritised view of where the business is absent, misunderstood or poorly evidenced, and what could reasonably be changed.

Because AI answers can vary by prompt, model, geography and time, measurement needs repeatable query sets and careful interpretation rather than pretending every response is deterministic.

## AEO content restructure

Good AEO content is still good content for people.

We restructure existing material where important answers are buried, ambiguous or disconnected from the entities and evidence that give them meaning. That can include clearer definitions, direct answers, stronger heading structure, better supporting evidence, useful comparisons, explicit relationships between concepts and more coherent internal linking.

The objective is extractability without turning the page into robotic fragments written for machines.

Where the required answer does not exist, we identify the content gap rather than stretching an unrelated page to cover it.

## Technical AEO

Technical AEO is about making useful information accessible and interpretable, not sprinkling schema everywhere.

Work can include crawl and indexation checks, structured data where it accurately represents visible content, entity consistency, canonicalisation, internal linking, source attribution and reviewing whether robots directives unintentionally block systems the business wants to reach.

Structured data can help machines understand a page. It does not guarantee a citation.

We keep technical recommendations tied to an observable problem rather than treating every possible markup type as a requirement.

## Citation tracking and AEO reporting

AI visibility is less mature and less deterministic than traditional rank tracking, so reporting needs to acknowledge that.

We define a repeatable set of commercially relevant questions and monitor whether the business appears, how it is described, which sources are cited and how patterns change. Where tools can automate useful parts of that process, we use them. Where human review is needed, we keep it.

AEO reporting should sit beside SEO, referral, direct, assisted conversion and pipeline data where available. Being mentioned by an AI system is interesting. Changing buyer behaviour is more useful.

## AEO services FAQ

### How are AEO services different from SEO services?

SEO focuses heavily on discoverability and performance in search engines. AEO adds the question of how content, entities and evidence are interpreted and surfaced inside AI-generated answers. The disciplines overlap substantially, so Commview treats AEO as an extension of modern search strategy rather than an excuse to duplicate the same work.

### How long until we get cited in AI answers?

There is no guaranteed timeline. AI systems choose and synthesise sources differently, their indexes and models change, and a technically correct page does not earn an automatic citation. We establish a baseline, improve the strongest controllable signals and track a repeatable query set so changes can be assessed over time.

### Which AI engines do you optimise for?

We design for the underlying behaviours shared across AI-assisted discovery rather than promising a secret formula for one model. Depending on the audience, measurement can include systems such as ChatGPT, Google AI experiences, Perplexity and Claude. Coverage should follow where your buyers actually research and what can be measured responsibly.
