## What does a fractional CMO do?

A fractional CMO gives a business access to senior marketing leadership for part of the time and for as long as the role is useful.

The title matters less than the responsibility.

The work can include positioning, ICP, go-to-market strategy, demand generation, channel investment, pipeline, measurement, team priorities, agency management, sales alignment and deciding where the next pound should go.

Sometimes the problem sits squarely inside marketing. Sometimes marketing is where a product, proposition, sales or operating problem becomes visible.

We start by finding the constraint.

## When fractional CMO support makes sense

You may not need a permanent CMO yet. You may still need CMO-level decisions.

Fractional leadership can make sense when:

- founders are still carrying senior marketing decisions
- an existing marketing team needs experienced leadership and clearer priorities
- growth has slowed and adding more activity is not fixing it
- GTM investment is increasing without enough confidence in where it should go
- product, marketing and sales are operating to different assumptions
- the business is entering a new market or launching a new proposition
- you need to understand what the permanent marketing organisation should become before hiring it

The objective is not to keep a fractional role forever. It is to give the business the leadership it needs for the stage it is in.

## Fractional CMO or full-time CMO?

A full-time CMO makes sense when the business has a persistent executive role, enough scope to justify it and clarity about the person it needs.

A fractional CMO can make more sense when the business needs senior leadership now but the role, organisation or GTM model is still changing.

It can also reduce the risk of hiring a permanent executive around assumptions that have not yet been tested.

The question is not whether fractional is inherently better than permanent. It is what level of leadership the business needs now and what structure it is ready to support.

## Fractional CMO or marketing agency?

They solve different problems.

An agency is normally engaged to deliver a defined marketing capability or channel. A fractional CMO sits closer to the business decisions: what should be prioritised, why, how different activities connect and what needs to change when the evidence says the plan is not working.

Commview can also bring execution capability around the strategy, but the leadership role remains accountable for the whole commercial picture rather than optimising one channel in isolation.

## Fractional CMO FAQ

### How much time does a fractional CMO spend in the business?

It depends on the problem and the level of ownership required. A focused GTM challenge may need less embedded time than leading an existing marketing function through several planning and delivery cycles. We define the responsibility and working rhythm first rather than forcing every business into the same number of days.

### How much does a fractional CMO cost?

There is no useful universal price because the scope can range from focused senior support to embedded leadership across a marketing function. Cost should reflect the responsibility, time and execution required. We scope the work around what needs to change rather than selling a fixed package regardless of the business.

### Can a fractional CMO work with our existing marketing team?

Yes. An existing team is often exactly why fractional leadership is useful. The role can provide senior direction, priorities, coaching, budget decisions and cross-functional alignment while the existing team continues to own its specialist work. Where capability is missing, Commview can bring additional operators around the problem.

### Can a fractional CMO work with our agencies?

Yes. A fractional CMO can set priorities, improve briefs, connect agency activity to the wider GTM plan and hold partners accountable to useful outcomes. The objective is not to replace good agencies. It is to make sure specialist work contributes to the same commercial system.

### When should we hire a permanent CMO instead?

A permanent CMO makes sense when the executive responsibility is persistent, the organisation is ready for the role and you understand the leadership profile required. Fractional support is useful when you need senior decisions now, want to test the shape of the role or do not yet need a full-time executive.
