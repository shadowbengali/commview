# Fractional CMO for Startups

You do not need a big marketing department to find out whether your go-to-market works. You need enough senior marketing leadership to make the important decisions, build the first repeatable motion and avoid spending your runway learning lessons somebody else has already learned.

Commview provides fractional CMO support for startups that need senior GTM leadership without making the full-time hire yet. We work across positioning, ICP, routes to market, demand generation, pipeline and the operating rhythm that connects them.

## What startups need from a fractional CMO

At startup stage, the job is usually foundational rather than optimisation.

The questions are basic, but expensive to get wrong. Who are we really for? Which problem are we solving? Why should that buyer choose us? Which route to market deserves the next pound? What evidence tells us the motion is beginning to work?

A startup fractional CMO should help answer those questions and turn the answers into activity. That can mean tightening positioning, narrowing the ICP, building the first demand programme, defining how marketing and sales work together or deciding what not to spend money on yet.

Commview starts with the constraint or question, not a pre-packaged marketing plan.

## When is a startup ready for a fractional CMO?

There is no universal funding round that makes a startup ready. The more useful test is whether marketing decisions have become important enough to need senior ownership, but a permanent CMO is still difficult to justify.

Common signals include:

- the founders are still making every marketing decision
- there is early customer evidence, but no repeatable route to pipeline
- different channels are being tested without a clear thesis
- positioning changes depending on who is selling
- a marketing hire or small team needs experienced leadership
- the business is about to increase GTM spend and wants to avoid learning through waste

For funded startups, this often becomes relevant after seed, but readiness matters more than the label on the funding round.

## What a Commview fractional CMO engagement looks like at startup stage

The scope should fit the stage of the company. We are not going to install enterprise marketing machinery into a business that still needs to prove its route to market.

We normally start by diagnosing the commercial problem and the evidence already available. From there, the work can include ICP and segmentation, positioning, messaging, channel selection, demand generation, pipeline definitions, measurement, sales handoff and the weekly operating rhythm needed to keep learning.

Where specialist execution is required, Commview can bring the right operator around the problem rather than asking one person to pretend they are an expert in every channel.

The objective is not more marketing activity. It is a clearer GTM system and enough evidence to know what deserves more investment.

## Fractional CMO cost for a startup

Fractional CMO cost should reflect the problem, the level of involvement and the amount of execution required.

A startup that needs a senior operator for a focused positioning and GTM problem is different from a business asking somebody to run the marketing function several days a week. We scope around what needs to change rather than forcing every startup into the same retainer.

The commercial test is simple: the engagement needs to make more sense than either hiring senior leadership too early or continuing to spend without enough senior direction.

## Startup fractional CMO FAQ

### How much runway do I need to hire a fractional CMO?

There is no sensible universal runway figure. You need enough runway to act on the decisions the engagement produces, not just pay for advice. If the business cannot fund any meaningful GTM activity afterwards, senior marketing leadership alone will not solve the constraint. We will say that before recommending an engagement.

### Should I hire a full-time head of marketing instead?

Hire full-time when the role is clear, the workload is persistent and the business can support the right level of person. A fractional CMO makes more sense when you need senior judgement now, the model is still evolving, or an existing team needs leadership before you know what permanent structure to build.
