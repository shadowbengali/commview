# Fractional CMO for B2B Scale-ups

Scale-ups rarely need more marketing for the sake of it. They need to understand why the motion that got them here is no longer producing the same result.

Commview provides fractional CMO leadership for B2B scale-ups that have customers, a working product and an established team, but need senior GTM leadership to unlock the next stage without immediately adding another permanent executive.

## Where scale-ups hit the growth plateau

Growth gets harder as the business changes.

The founder-led motion stops scaling. A channel that once performed becomes expensive. The addressable audience broadens and the message becomes vague. Marketing generates activity but sales questions the quality. Teams add tools, campaigns and people while the commercial system becomes harder to see.

There are often recognisable plateaus as companies move through different revenue stages, but there is no magic £2m, £10m or £25m diagnosis. The cause matters more than the number.

The job is to find the constraint before prescribing the fix.

## What a scale-up fractional CMO actually does

At scale-up stage, Commview is usually improving and connecting an existing system rather than creating marketing from zero.

That can include repositioning the proposition, tightening the ICP, changing channel investment, redesigning demand generation, improving pipeline visibility, fixing sales handoff, defining measurement or introducing a clearer operating rhythm across GTM.

Sometimes the answer is more demand. Sometimes it is better conversion. Sometimes the business is targeting the wrong accounts, carrying a proposition that no longer differentiates, or asking marketing to compensate for a product or sales problem.

We work across those boundaries because the problems do too.

## How a fractional CMO fits with your existing marketing team

The aim is leverage, not replacement.

A fractional CMO can give an existing Head of Marketing, demand team or specialist agencies clearer priorities, faster decisions and senior air cover. The work can include coaching, planning, budget decisions, cross-functional alignment and direct involvement in the problems that need experienced judgement.

Where the team already has the capability, we use it. Where a specialist gap exists, Commview can bring the right operator into the engagement.

## Scale-up fractional CMO FAQ

### At what ARR does a scale-up typically need a fractional CMO?

ARR alone is a poor trigger. The stronger signal is that GTM decisions have become strategically important, the existing team needs senior leadership, and a permanent CMO is either premature or unnecessarily rigid. A scale-up can reach that point at very different revenue levels depending on market, sales motion and team.

### Fractional CMO or promoting our existing marketing lead?

Promote the internal lead when they are ready to own the strategic, commercial and organisational parts of the role, not simply because they know the company. A fractional CMO can also work alongside that person, giving them support and experience while the business tests what the senior role actually needs to become.

### How long does a typical scale-up engagement last?

It depends on the constraint. A focused GTM problem can be addressed over a shorter engagement, while embedded leadership usually lasts longer because decisions, execution and measurement need to run through several cycles. We define the outcome and working rhythm first rather than selling a fixed-duration fractional CMO package.
