# SaaS SEO Consultant

SaaS SEO has to understand the product as well as the keyword.

Prospects search for categories, features, integrations, use cases, competitors, alternatives and answers to the problem your software solves. Treat all of those queries as generic blog opportunities and you can grow traffic without making the product easier to discover or buy.

Commview builds SaaS SEO around the product, the buyer and the commercial journey.

## SaaS SEO is a specialism, not a variant

A SaaS website is part product catalogue, part education layer, part comparison tool and part sales environment.

That creates search opportunities that do not exist in the same way for many other businesses. Feature pages can capture specific capability demand. Integration pages can connect your product to an existing stack. Alternative and comparison pages can appear when a buyer is actively shortlisting. Use-case pages can translate functionality into a recognisable problem.

A SaaS SEO consultant needs to understand how those page types fit together rather than defaulting to a publishing calendar full of informational articles.

## The four layers of a SaaS SEO strategy

### Product

Category, feature, integration and use-case pages should make the product understandable to search engines, AI systems and buyers. These pages sit closest to the commercial proposition.

### Comparison

Alternative, versus and comparison content serves buyers who are already evaluating a market. It needs to be useful and defensible, not a thin page designed only to capture a competitor's name.

### Educational

Problem-led and category education creates earlier demand and builds topical depth. It should connect back to the product rather than becoming a disconnected media operation.

### Community and evidence

Customer questions, implementation knowledge, proof, documentation and useful expert material create the depth buyers increasingly use when validating software, including through AI-assisted research.

The mix depends on the product and market. It is not a fixed template.

## SaaS SEO FAQ

### How is SaaS SEO different from B2B SEO?

SaaS SEO is often a subset of B2B SEO, but the product creates additional search surfaces such as features, integrations, alternatives, comparisons and use cases. SaaS companies also need to connect educational demand to product adoption. The strategy should reflect how software is researched, evaluated, trialled and retained.

### Should we build comparison pages against our competitors?

Sometimes. Comparison pages are useful when buyers genuinely search for the comparison and your page can help them make a decision. They should explain meaningful differences, use supportable claims and acknowledge where alternatives fit. Thin pages that simply declare your product better are unlikely to build much buyer trust.

### Do integration pages actually rank for SaaS?

They can when there is real search demand and the integration has enough substance to answer the query. A useful integration page explains what connects, what the combined workflow enables, who it is for and how it works. Generating hundreds of near-identical pages without genuine integrations is a different strategy entirely.
