# Fractional CMO UK

Commview provides UK-based fractional CMO leadership for B2B businesses that need experienced GTM direction without making another permanent senior hire.

We are based in Manchester and work with businesses across the UK and internationally. The model is deliberately flexible: remote where that makes work faster, in person where being in the room materially improves the outcome.

## UK-based fractional CMO with global experience

Being UK-based does not mean the experience stops at the UK border.

Our background includes building and running GTM, product and digital programmes across multiple European and international markets. That matters when a UK scale-up is deciding whether a problem is local, whether a proposition travels, or how much of its GTM model can be repeated in another market.

Commview combines that experience with the practicality of working in the same market and time zone as UK leadership teams.

## B2B fractional CMO experience in the UK

Our experience spans telecoms and cybersecurity, industrial distribution, retail, digital products and B2B services.

The common thread is not the sector. It is the commercial problem: positioning that has stopped differentiating, pipeline that is not converting, marketing investment without enough evidence, a GTM model that needs to scale, or teams that need stronger senior coordination.

That is why we start with the problem rather than selling a sector playbook.

## Fractional CMO in London, Manchester and remotely

Commview is Manchester-based. We can work with teams in Manchester, London and elsewhere in the UK, with remote collaboration used for the work that does not benefit from travel.

In-person time is useful for work such as leadership sessions, positioning workshops, difficult cross-functional decisions and periods where being embedded with the team speeds things up. It should have a reason, not become theatre.

For ongoing delivery, a mix of remote and in-person work is usually more efficient.

## UK B2B fractional CMO FAQ

### Do you only work with UK clients?

No. Commview is based in Manchester and works primarily with B2B businesses, but the model can support international businesses and multi-market work. Our experience includes GTM and digital programmes across the UK, Europe and wider international markets, so geography does not need to dictate the engagement.

### Do you meet clients in person?

Yes, where it improves the work. Workshops, leadership decisions and periods of embedded delivery can benefit from being in the room. Other work is usually faster remotely. We agree the working model around the engagement rather than requiring clients to buy a fixed number of office days.

### Do I need to be based in London to hire a fractional CMO?

No. Commview is Manchester-based and can work with businesses across the UK. Location matters far less than access to the right stakeholders, data and decisions. Where in-person work is useful, we agree when and where it will make the biggest difference.
