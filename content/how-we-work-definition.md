## INSIGHT

H2:
**Insight: find out what's actually going on**

The problem you are seeing is not always the problem you have.

Poor leads might actually be weak positioning. Low conversion might be the wrong audience. Slow product adoption might start with what was built rather than how it was marketed. A capacity problem might be a process that should never have remained manual.

So we start with evidence.

Depending on the problem, that could mean customer behaviour, search data, pipeline, product usage, sales feedback, analytics, workflows, systems or conversations with the people closest to the work.

We follow the evidence until we understand where the constraint is.

Pull line:
**The output isn't a deck. It's a decision about what needs to change.**

## MOMENTUM

H2:
**Momentum: get something moving**

Once we know where the problem sits, we start doing something about it.

That might mean changing the positioning. Reworking the customer journey. Building a demand programme. Prototyping a product change. Fixing measurement. Automating a workflow. Or stopping something that is not working.

This is where Commview is deliberately different.

**Strategy and execution don't get handed to different people.**

The person helping make the strategic decision stays close enough to the execution to see whether it works.

When specialist expertise is needed, we bring it into the work.

## IMPACT

H2:
**Impact: prove it made a difference**

Activity is not the outcome.

Before changing something, we establish what we are trying to improve and how we will know whether it is working.

Then we measure it.

If the evidence says it is working, we build on it.

If it does not, we change it.

Pull line:
**The point isn't to prove the strategy was right. It's to make the business better.**

## What working with Commview actually looks like

H2:
**A working rhythm, not a rigid methodology**

This section should make the operating model tangible without pretending every engagement follows an identical timetable.

### Understand

### Diagnose

### Do
Start shipping work rather than spending months planning it.

### Measure
See what changed, learn from it and decide what moves next.

## Different problems need different engagements

H2:
**One way of working. Different ways to use it.**

You do not always need a consultant embedded in the business for six months. And some problems are not going to be solved in a two-week project.

The way we work depends on the problem.

### Fractional leadership
Senior ownership embedded in the business when you need ongoing leadership without making a permanent senior hire.

### Focused projects
A defined commercial problem with a clear outcome, such as positioning, product discovery, SEO strategy or an AI implementation.

### Build and embed
We help create the capability, process or system, get it working and help your team take ownership of it.

### Specialist support
When the problem requires deeper expertise, we bring the right specialist into the work rather than pretending one person can do everything.

## FAQ

H2:
**Working with Commview: frequently asked questions**

Use these five questions and answers. Keep answers direct and around 40–60 words.

### How does Commview work with clients?

We start by understanding the problem, the evidence and what is happening inside the business. From there, we identify what needs to change, help execute it and measure whether it made a difference. The exact approach depends on the problem rather than following a fixed consulting methodology.

### Does Commview provide strategy or execution?

Both. Strategy and execution stay connected. The person helping diagnose the problem and decide what needs to change stays close to the work required to make it happen. Where specialist expertise is needed, we bring the right people into the work.

### How long does a Commview engagement last?

It depends on the problem. Some work is a focused project with a defined outcome, while fractional leadership or more complex transformation may require ongoing involvement. We agree the scope and expected outcomes before the work starts rather than forcing every problem into the same engagement model.

### Can Commview work with our existing team or agency?

Yes. We can work alongside internal marketing, sales, product and technology teams as well as existing agencies and specialist partners. Often our role is to connect the work across those functions, establish what matters commercially and help everyone work towards the same outcome.

### How do you measure whether the work has been successful?

We establish what needs to change and how we will measure it before getting deep into execution. The measure depends on the problem, whether that is pipeline, conversion, product adoption, efficiency, cost, capacity or another commercial outcome. If the evidence shows something is not working, we change it.
