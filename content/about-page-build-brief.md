# About page build brief

## Source of truth

Build the Commview `/about` page from this content brief.

The user will provide the approved visual design separately to Claude. Treat that supplied design as the visual source of truth. Reuse the existing Commview header, footer, typography, spacing system and responsive patterns rather than inventing a new design language.

This brief is the source of truth for **content, structure, claims and page behaviour**.

## What the page must communicate

Commview is **not Asad Ali operating alone under a company name**.

Commview is an operator-led network that gives businesses access to experienced people across GTM, growth, product, AI, transformation, commercial, finance, cybersecurity and related disciplines.

The central proposition is:

**Commview gives businesses access to people who have already done the work.**

The network includes experience gained at organisations including Vodafone, Google, Microsoft, Apple, Capgemini, Accenture, Barclays, Travis Perkins, Resideo, ADI Global and Distrelec.

Do not imply those companies endorse, partner with or are clients of Commview unless separately evidenced. The logos represent organisations where people in the Commview network have gained relevant experience.

The page should explain **why the network model exists**, not simply display impressive company names.

## Tone

- Direct, experienced, human UK English.
- Operator-led, not consultancy jargon.
- No em dashes anywhere in visible copy.
- Avoid generic claims such as “world-class talent”, “best-in-class”, “industry-leading” or “at the forefront”.
- Show credibility through concrete experience rather than adjectives.
- Do not imply Commview has a large permanent team.
- Do not describe the network as an advisory board.
- Do not call people “resources”.
- Do not create a fake Meet the Team grid.
- Do not invent names, job titles or biographies for network members.
- Do not turn the page into Asad's CV.
